I packaged the whole process as a reusable Claude skill: it checks the picture, writes the prompt for the relief and its three maps, and sets up the lighting. Download it above.

Holo card

Kari, an original character of mine, as a holographic trading card. The picture is five layers: the background, Kari, the sparks, her line art and the gold frame. They sit at different depths, so as the card tilts they slide past one another while the frame stays put. The rainbow foil isn’t painted on: it is worked out from the angle you look at it from, so it moves when you do. Built as a Blender scene, exported as real card geometry and lit again in the browser. Click it to turn it over.

2026 · Blender → Three.js · five layers, one foil
